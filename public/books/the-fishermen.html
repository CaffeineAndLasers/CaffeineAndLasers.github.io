The Fishermen

by Chigozie Obioma

Rating: 🟧

Type: Fiction

Read: July 2026

My Thoughts

Sigh I wish that I enjoyed this one. This was another book I read for my book club and it just did not grip me. Let me start off on a positive. The Prose. Obioma's prose is great IMO, I had my gripes with the plot, but the way Obioma writes, the musicality of the dialogue, the childlike perspectiveo on the world does make it enjoyable to read on a moment to moment basis. Unfortunately, the narrative itself just did not click with me.

After finishing it myself and being a little dissapointed, I read some other reviews which compared it to a Tragedy, and thats when the story clicked for me. If you read it like a Greek tragedy I imagine that the narrative may be more satisfying.

Unfortunately, that reading was not super obvious in the text itself.

EARLY STORY SPOILERS FROM HERE ON

I think the reason the narrative falls apart for me, is that Abulu was never a credible prophet for me in the way the witches of Macbeth or the Delphic Oracle was in Oedepus. When I saw Abulu, all I saw was a crackhead, engauging in average crackhead behaviour. So I think without that central pillar to support the narrative, the rest of the tragedy collapses in on itself.
