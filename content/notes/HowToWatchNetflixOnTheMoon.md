Do you know how we communicated with the Artemis II mission? All that crispy high definition video? When I can barely get 5G on Earth, how did they get such a good connection to stream the footage live?

I am finally getting around to sharing some of my work on this site. Last week I delivered a presentaiton ahead of Science Week detailing how my lab participated in the Artemis II mission. While my role in the mission was tiny, I was still very proud to deliver this talk on behalf of the team who actually did all the work.
