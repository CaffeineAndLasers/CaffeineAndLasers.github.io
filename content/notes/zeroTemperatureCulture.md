[Lauren Leek's Fantastic Essay](https://laurenleek.substack.com/p/temperature-zero-for-culture-why) explores why in the age of personalized algorithms for everyone, everything feels so samey. 

In her words:

> "We have never had more individual data and naive intuition says all that granular personal data should fragment us, splinter the world into a billion niches. And yet the opposite is happening. The more the machines learn about us as individuals, the more alike our worlds look."

And to summarize her entire essay with a FANTASTIC quote from her:

> "Personalisation under a standard loss function is regression to the collective mean with extra steps."

That explains so much about the modern world it is uncanny! Read the Essay
