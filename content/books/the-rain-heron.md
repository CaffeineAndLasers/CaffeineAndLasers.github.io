Boy I really enjoyed this one. This was another book club pick, and so far it has been my favourite of all the book club picks. The interplay between characters and their environment is compelling, the dramatic irony is gripping, and the full story is filled with a sense of loss. 

He makes a lot of interesting decisions as well, the inciting incident, the politics which has thrust the characters into conflict is never actually explained. We see civil war, resource conflict and authoritarian control as it appears to the pawns. It turns out a boots ideology doesn't change how it feels on your neck. 

Fantastic book, highly reccomend.
