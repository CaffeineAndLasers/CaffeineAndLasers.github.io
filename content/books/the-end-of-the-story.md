Man. This was... not good. 

I am willing ot say it is entirely possible I am just too stupid to enjoy this book. Seriously I could not find any enjoyment in this. It is a story without any plot, about a relationship, without any dialogue, and a love story without any love, romance, sex or intimacy. And the story is told by an unreliable narrator, with a non-linear time scale, and neither the narrator nor the love interest are particularly likeable characters. 

If you are going to do even one of these, let alone ALL of them, you better have something important to say. Instead she want to tell you about walking up (and down) the hill, and muse about whether she ordered the chicken or the fish that day many years ago. 

If i wasn't for my book club there is no way I would have finished this one.
