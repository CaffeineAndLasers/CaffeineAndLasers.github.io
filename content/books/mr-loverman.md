This was another book club pick, and I will admit, I was not excited for it. That said I was glad to be wrong. I quite enjoyed this one. 

When I saw a bit of the marketing around the novel, in a typical BBC fashion, the most interesting thing anyone had to say about it, was that it is about a Gay Carribian Boomer. While I am glad that diverse stories are being told, gay Carribians included, I often like a little bit of plot with my identity politics 🤷

Well Evaristo delivered. The persoanalities of the central cast are so magnetic, that even when they are succumbing to their character flaws, you still can't help but laugh and wish them the best. I really like a book which can write a flawed protagonist, show how others suffer from their actions, but also make you wish the best for them. You really want Barry to learn his lesson, not to punish him, but so that him and everyone around him can finally be happy. 

I listened to the audiobook version, and the voice actors performance really breathed life into the characters.
