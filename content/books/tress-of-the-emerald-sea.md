I adore Brandon Sanderson, so any review will be hopelessly compromised. 

This book shows off Sanderson's expertise and creativity at world building. I want to say like no other, but it is just like Sanderson he is so consistent. 

The book has fun inverting the tropes, our heroic girl has to go save the charming prince (after discussing the matter with her parents of course -- as not nearly enough heros do beforehand). Then we get a fun pirate romp with a talking animal sidekick. This was also the first time I have seen Sanderson make a penis joke. How very PG13 of him. I am not sure how many times has done that but it got an unexpected chuckle from me. 

I really appreciated how he handled the topic of disability in this. The book has a deaf character, who Sanderson goes out of his way to explain CAN lipread, but prefers to use an ~~iPad~~ ✨*magical slate*✨ to communicate, because lipreading is **really damn tiring**. And there is a GREAT bit which I wont spoil here, which turns a running gag into something kind of heartbreaking realization.

Anyway, pirates, talking animal sidekicks, nice disability representation and a deep well explored world. What more could you ask for?
